About Us

We do the filing so you can do the founding.

SNUPA is a Botswana-based consultancy built around one idea: starting and running a compliant business shouldn't require a law degree, or a week off work spent in government queues.

SNUPA was founded after watching too many good business ideas stall — not from lack of demand, but from a rejected CIPA filing, a missed VAT deadline, or a license nobody knew they needed.

We started by helping a handful of small traders in Palapye register properly. Word spread, mostly because we picked up the phone and actually explained what each form meant, rather than just filing it and moving on.

Today SNUPA supports founders across Botswana — from first‑time entrepreneurs registering their very first company, to established businesses that need an ongoing company secretary and tax agent they can rely on.

We're not a call centre and we're not a law firm. We're a small, hands‑on consultancy that treats your registration file the way we'd want our own handled: correctly, on time, and with someone who picks up the phone when you have a question.

“Our mission is simple: make it possible for any Motswana with a good idea to start a fully compliant business — without needing to understand CIPA or BURS themselves.”
— SNUPA Founding Principle

Process

How a file moves through SNUPA.

Typical turnaround: a new company registration usually takes about 1 week. PPADB codes follow their own timeline — new codes are typically approved in about 1 week, while adding further codes to an existing PPADB registration takes around 21 days.
